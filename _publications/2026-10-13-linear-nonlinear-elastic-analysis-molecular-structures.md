This work presents an exact atomistic formulation for the elastic analysis of molecular structures, encompassing both linear and nonlinear regimes. The methodology is based on the direct use of geometry and potential energy, explicitly incorporating non-bonded interactions. This approach allows for a rigorous treatment of molecular elasticity, bridging the gap between atomistic detail and continuum-level mechanical properties.
